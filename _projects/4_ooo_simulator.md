**Advisor:** Prof. Moinuddin K. Qureshi, Georgia Tech &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Aug 2024 – Dec 2024**

A lightweight, cycle-accurate, **4-way-wide out-of-order** CPU simulator built in C++.

- Modeled a multi-level cache hierarchy and replacement policies (LRU, LFU, and more).
- Implemented **static** (way-partitioning) and **dynamic** (utility-based) cache partitioning schemes.
