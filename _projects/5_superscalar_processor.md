**Advisor:** Prof. Virendra Singh, IIT Bombay &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Sep 2022 – Jan 2023**

Led a four-member team to design a **2-wide superscalar out-of-order processor**.

- Designed the logic for a 2-bit branch predictor, Fetch, Decode, Reservation Station,
  and ROB with **register renaming**.
- Implemented and tested the complete design in **VHDL** on Quartus Prime.
