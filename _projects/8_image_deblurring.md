**Advisor:** Prof. Amit Sethi, IIT Bombay &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Feb 2023 – Apr 2023**

A deep convolutional neural network that restores blurred images by reversing the effects
of motion blur.

- Applied **transfer learning** on a dataset of **12,240 image patches** to reduce noise and
  artifacts and accelerate convergence by fine-tuning a pre-trained CNN.
- Achieved up to **92% deblurring accuracy** with a model built in **TensorFlow**.
