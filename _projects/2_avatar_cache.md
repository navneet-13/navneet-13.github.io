**Advisors:** Prof. Moinuddin K. Qureshi (Georgia Tech), Prof. Biswabandan Panda (IIT Bombay)
&nbsp;·&nbsp; **Jun 2024 – Aug 2025**

Avatar Cache is a morphable cache design that delivers security **on demand** at
state-of-the-art cache design complexity. Users can opt out of security to avoid its
performance and power overheads, motivated by the Mirage cache's use of extra ways for
security combined with high associativity to reduce overheads.

- Implemented on the **ChampSim** multi-core simulator.
- In secure mode: **0.2%** performance overhead, **2%** power overhead, and **0.9%** storage overhead.
